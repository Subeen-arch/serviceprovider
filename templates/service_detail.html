What affects the ranking

When you search, every provider is scored out of 100 against four factors. By default they count {{ weights.distance }} / {{ weights.rating }} / {{ weights.price }} / {{ weights.experience }}, and you can choose which one matters most.

Distance · {{ weights.distance }} Road distance from their shop to your pin, found with Dijkstra's algorithm over the valley junction graph.
Rating · {{ weights.rating }} The mean of reviews left by customers after a completed job.
Price · {{ weights.price }} Their hourly rate against the cheapest provider shown. A rate above a budget you set scores zero.
Experience · {{ weights.experience }} Years in the trade, as declared on the provider's profile.
