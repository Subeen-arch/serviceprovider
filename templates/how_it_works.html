How it works

From “I need help” to a booked local provider.

Nothing important is hidden behind a black box. Here is what happens at each step and what the application does with your answer.

Step 1

Say what you need

Choose a service category so the application knows which approved providers should be considered.

Behind the scenes: the active service id filters approved and available providers in MySQL.
Step 2

Drop your pin on the map

Choose the job location. The address is stored together with latitude and longitude, and locations outside the Kathmandu valley are rejected.

Behind the scenes: Galli Maps + server-side coordinate validation.
Step 3

Compare ranked providers

Providers are scored using distance, rating, price and experience. You can inspect the factor breakdown instead of trusting a hidden ranking.

Behind the scenes: weighted recommendation scoring over four factors.
Step 4

See the real road route

Open a provider and the route from the job location to the provider is calculated through the road network rather than with a straight line.

Behind the scenes: Dijkstra shortest-path routing over the Kathmandu junction graph.
Step 5

Choose a time and send the request

The requested date, time and duration are checked against the provider's working hours and against live bookings for the same slot.

Behind the scenes: provider availability rules + database double-booking protection.
Step 6

Pay after they accept

The provider can accept or reject. Once accepted, the customer pays through Khalti; after completion, the customer can leave a review.

Behind the scenes: Khalti ePayment initiate + lookup, then booking and review status updates.
